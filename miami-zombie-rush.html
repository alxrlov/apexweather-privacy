Miami Zombie Rush Privacy Policy

Apex Interactive Studio develops Miami Zombie Rush.

Data and local storage

The game runs offline and does not require an account. It stores progress, in-game currency and preferences on your device. The game does not transmit this information off your device. It does not use advertising or analytics SDKs and does not collect or share personal data. Local game data stays on your device until you clear it. You can clear the local save by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstalling the game.

Support

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond to your request and keep it only as needed to handle the conversation or meet applicable legal obligations. Our email provider processes messages to deliver and store them. Access to the support mailbox is restricted. You can contact us to ask about or request deletion of your support correspondence.
